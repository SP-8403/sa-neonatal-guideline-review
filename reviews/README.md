# South African Neonatal Guideline – Structured Technical Review

** Reviewer:** Shakti Pillay
Neonatologist
University of Cape Town  

**Review Commenced:** 30 October 2025

---

This repository documents the structured technical review and harmonisation of draft chapters of the South African Neonatal Guidelines.

The purpose of this review process is to:

- Strengthen structural clarity  
- Improve clinical safety  
- Align content with South African district and regional practice  
- Standardise terminology and formatting  
- Enhance governance and referral clarity  
- Reduce medico-legal ambiguity  
- Support consistent implementation across levels of care  

---

## Review Framework

Each chapter is reviewed using a structured approach:

1. Structural Assessment  
2. Clinical Safety Review  
3. Governance and Escalation Clarity  
4. Feasibility in South African Context  
5. Formatting and Internal Consistency  
6. Harmonised Clean Version  

This process reflects structured clinical oversight and technical refinement.
