## Overview

This repository documents the structured **Technical Review and Editorial Harmonisation** of draft chapters of the South African Neonatal Guidelines following extensive **National Consultation**.

The review process incorporates feedback from clinicians and stakeholders across **South Africa**, including contributors from multiple provinces, institutions, and levels of care. Comments received during the consultation process included both attributed and anonymous submissions.

The purpose of this repository is to transparently document the structured synthesis of this national input and the editorial work undertaken to strengthen the clarity, safety, and usability of the guideline.

---

## Purpose of the Review

The technical review process aims to:

- Strengthen structural clarity of guideline chapters  
- Improve clinical safety and reduce ambiguity  
- Align recommendations with **South African District and Regional Clinical Practice and Care**  
- Standardise terminology and formatting across chapters  
- Enhance governance and referral pathway clarity  
- Reduce medico-legal ambiguity in clinical guidance  
- Support consistent implementation across levels of neonatal care across the country.

---

## National Input and Consultation

The chapters reviewed in this repository reflect **Extensive National Input** received during the consultation process.

Reviewer comments were carefully reviewed and synthesised to ensure that the revised chapters reflect:

- Different healthcare settings in South Africa  
- Clinical safety across District, Regional, and Tertiary levels of care  
- Clear governance and referral pathways within the national health system  
- Harmonised terminology and consistent clinical guidance.

This process represents a structured effort to integrate **Broad National Clinical Expertise** into the final guideline chapters.

---

## Visual and Implementation Deliverables


To support guideline-quality figures and implementation-ready algorithms, **licensed visual assets were personally obtained through paid image licensing** to ensure appropriate usage rights.

Clinical diagrams and algorithms were **redrawn and standardised by the technical reviewer** using a reproducible diagramming framework (**Mermaid** and **Drawio**), enabling:

- Consistent visual styling across chapters  
- Clear, straight-line clinical pathways  
- Clean **SVG export** suitable for print and PDF production  
- Version-controlled diagram updates aligned with national governance needs.

These visual assets form part of the technical refinement undertaken during the review process.

---


## Review Framework

All national reviewer comments were consolidated into structured review logs and assessed against:

- Clinical Safety  
- Governance and Escalation Clarity  
- Feasibility Across Levels of Care  
- National Implementation Standards.

Each chapter is reviewed using the following structured framework:

- Structural Assessment  
- Clinical Safety Review  
- Governance and Escalation Clarity  
- Feasibility in the South African Context  
- Formatting and Internal Consistency  
- Production of a Harmonised Clean Version.
