# Respiratory Conditions – Technical Review Summary

This section documents the structured technical review of the **Respiratory Conditions** chapter of the South African Neonatal Care Guidelines.

Reviewer comments were received from clinicians and stakeholders participating in the national consultation process. Feedback included both attributed and anonymous comments submitted during the review phase.

## Review Process

All reviewer feedback was carefully reviewed and synthesised. The purpose of the technical review was to:

- improve structural clarity of the chapter
- enhance clinical safety and usability
- align recommendations with current South African neonatal practice
- clarify referral and escalation pathways
- standardise terminology and formatting across the guideline.

Where multiple reviewers raised similar concerns, comments were grouped and synthesised to guide revisions to the chapter.

## Key Themes Emerging from Reviewer Feedback

### Definitions and Terminology
Reviewers requested clearer distinctions between:

- apnoea of prematurity
- periodic breathing
- central, obstructive and mixed apnoea.

Terminology related to respiratory distress and respiratory failure was standardised to improve consistency across the guideline.

### Structure and Readability
Several sections contained dense narrative text. Reviewer feedback suggested:

- restructuring sections into clearer clinical guidance
- improving the layout of diagnostic and management tables
- simplifying procedural descriptions for frontline clinicians.

### Referral and Escalation of Care
Comments highlighted the need for clearer guidance on when babies should be:

- referred to higher levels of neonatal care
- discussed with specialist centres
- considered for intubation or mechanical ventilation.

These pathways were clarified to align with South African referral structures.

### Respiratory Support Guidance
Reviewers requested clearer guidance regarding:

- initiation of CPAP
- oxygen saturation targets
- escalation of respiratory support
- indications for intubation.

The CPAP section was reorganised to provide clearer step-wise clinical guidance.

### Alignment with Current Clinical Practice
Reviewer feedback addressed the management of several key neonatal respiratory conditions, including:

- respiratory distress syndrome (RDS)
- transient tachypnoea of the newborn (TTN)
- congenital pneumonia
- meconium aspiration syndrome (MAS)
- pneumothorax
- persistent pulmonary hypertension of the newborn (PPHN).

Where appropriate, wording was revised to ensure consistency with current clinical practice and national care pathways.

## Summary of Editorial Changes

Following synthesis of reviewer feedback, the chapter was revised to:

- clarify definitions of neonatal apnoea
- improve the structure of respiratory distress assessment
- standardise terminology across respiratory conditions
- strengthen referral and escalation guidance
- reorganise respiratory support instructions
- simplify procedural descriptions.

These revisions aim to improve the clarity, consistency, and practical usability of the respiratory chapter for clinicians working across different levels of neonatal care.
