SCREENING PROCESS

Pulse oximetry screening for critical congenital heart disease

MEASUREMENT 1
Measure oxygen saturation in the right hand and either foot at 24 hours of life, or earlier if being discharged, or at presentation.
FAIL
Immediate action
Criteria
SpO₂ ≤ 89%
in either the right hand or foot
Action: Refer
Refer for immediate assessment.
INDETERMINATE / RETEST
Repeat measurement
Criteria
SpO₂ 90%–94%
in either right hand or foot
OR
difference ≥ 4% between right hand and foot
Action: Re-test
Repeat pulse oximetry in one hour.
MEASUREMENT 2
Repeat right-hand and foot oxygen saturation after one hour.
FAIL
Criteria
SpO₂ 90%–94%
or difference ≥ 4%
Action: Refer
Refer for assessment.
PASS
Criteria
SpO₂ ≥ 95%
in right hand and foot
and difference ≤ 3%
No further screening
Provide routine newborn care if well.
PASS
Screen complete
Criteria
SpO₂ ≥ 95%
in right hand and foot
AND
difference ≤ 3% between right hand and foot
No further screening
Provide routine newborn care if well.
Any failed screen requires clinical assessment and escalation through the local referral pathway.
