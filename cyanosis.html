ALGORITHM 1
INITIAL APPROACH TO CYANOSIS
STEP 1: IDENTIFY TYPE

CENTRAL CYANOSIS

Lips or tongue blue

Oxygen saturation
less than 95%

👄

PERIPHERAL CYANOSIS

Hands or feet blue
Lips or tongue pink

Oxygen saturation
greater than or
equal to 95%

✋ 🦶 🦶
Warm baby and observe
STEP 2: IMMEDIATE ASSESSMENT
🫁

STABILISE AIRWAY AND BREATHING

  • Start oxygen support to achieve target oxygen saturation 90% to 95%
  • If not responding, consider intubation
💧

CHECK GLUCOSE AND TEMPERATURE

  • Screen for low blood glucose and treat
  • Check temperature and keep warm
🖥

MONITOR

  • Pre- and post-ductal oxygen saturation
  • Heart rate, respirations, perfusion
  • Blood glucose
Proceed to Evaluation
(Algorithm 2)
ALGORITHM 2
EVALUATION OF CENTRAL CYANOSIS
STEP 3: CLINICAL ASSESSMENT
📋

HISTORY

  • Review antenatal scan for anomalies
  • Maternal conditions: diabetes, hypertension, infection
  • Birth history and resuscitation details
  • Maternal medication and infection risk
🩺

EXAMINATION

  • General: dysmorphic features, pallor, jaundice, oedema
  • Perfusion: capillary refill time, pulses, blood pressure
  • Cardiac: heart rate, rhythm, murmur
  • Respiratory: work of breathing, air entry
  • Neurological: tone, reflexes, seizures
📟

PULSE OXIMETRY SCREENING

  • Measure oxygen saturation in right hand (pre-ductal) and either foot (post-ductal)
  • Record difference

ABNORMAL IF:

Oxygen saturation less than 95% in air
OR
Pre-ductal and post-ductal difference greater than 3%

STEP 4: PERFORM HYPEROXIA TEST
🫁

Administer 100% oxygen for 10 minutes and reassess oxygen saturation.

ALGORITHM 3
DIFFERENTIATION AND MANAGEMENT
❤️
🫁

CARDIAC CAUSE
LIKELY IF:

  • Cyanosis persists despite adequate oxygen
  • Weak or absent pulses or poor perfusion
  • Murmur
  • Dysmorphic features
  • Shock or metabolic acidosis

NON-CARDIAC CAUSE
LIKELY IF:

  • Oxygen saturation improves with oxygen
  • Signs of respiratory distress or lung disease
  • Normal cardiac examination
  • No differential cyanosis: pre-ductal and post-ductal difference less than 3%
MANAGEMENT
☎

Urgently discuss with referral centre.

💉

Consider prostaglandin E1 in discussion with referral centre.

🩸

Obtain blood gas. Low oxygen tension is typical.

🚑

Arrange urgent transfer to cardiac centre.

MANAGEMENT
🫁

Provide respiratory support as required.

🛡

Treat underlying cause, including pneumonia, sepsis, respiratory distress syndrome or persistent pulmonary hypertension.

🖥

Monitor and reassess frequently.

🌡

Maintain normothermia and normoglycaemia.

📋

CONTINUE SUPPORTIVE CARE

Maintain target oxygen saturation 90% to 95% using pre-ductal monitoring, monitor vital signs, urine output and perfusion.

!
